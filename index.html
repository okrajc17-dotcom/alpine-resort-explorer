      Instructor counts are a snapshot of maisonsport.com's public resort list on 22 Oct 2026 (241 resorts with active instructors; this map shows 46 of the busiest in France, Switzerland and Italy). Villages in the same resort are listed separately because one instructor can teach in several.

      The Val Thorens 3D mountain uses a 150 m elevation grid from AWS Terrain Tiles, smoothed and raised 15% so the relief reads on a small screen. Runs and lifts are drawn from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), simplified to about 25 m. Lift names and positions are real; the three meeting points are examples placed at real lift stations, because in the real product each instructor sets their own. Altitudes, train stations and straight-line airport distances are indicative. Search buttons open the real Maison Sport results page in a new tab.
